# 📊 Superstore Business Intelligence Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Business Intelligence dashboard developed in Microsoft Power BI using the Superstore Sales dataset. The dashboard provides valuable insights into sales performance, customer behavior, profitability, and product performance to support data-driven business decisions.

---

## Business Objectives

- Monitor overall business performance.
- Analyze sales trends across regions and time.
- Evaluate customer purchasing behavior.
- Measure profitability across products and categories.
- Identify top-performing products and customer segments.

---

## Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- Star Schema Data Modeling
- Microsoft Excel

---

## Dashboard Pages

### Executive Overview
Provides a high-level summary of key business KPIs, including Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Orders, Total Customers, and Total Quantity Sold.

### Sales Analysis
Analyzes sales performance across regions, categories, customer segments, shipping modes, and monthly sales trends.

### Customer Analysis
Examines customer purchasing behavior, identifies top customers, and compares customer segments based on sales and order frequency.

### Profitability Analysis
Evaluates profit performance across products, regions, categories, and customer segments while assessing the impact of discounts on profitability.

### Product Analysis
Provides insights into product sales, quantity sold, and profitability to identify top-performing products and support inventory planning.

---

## Key Business Insights

- The business generated approximately **$2.30M** in total sales from over **5,000 orders**.
- The **Consumer** segment contributed the largest share of total sales.
- The **West** region achieved the highest sales and profit.
- **Technology** was the highest-performing product category.
- Sales increased toward the end of the year, indicating stronger business performance during the final quarter.
- Higher discount levels generally resulted in lower profitability.

---

## Recommendations

- Increase investment in high-performing product categories.
- Strengthen marketing efforts in lower-performing regions.
- Review discount strategies to improve profit margins.
- Implement customer loyalty programs to encourage repeat purchases.
- Maintain adequate inventory for top-selling products.
